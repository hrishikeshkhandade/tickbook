Harness:  the "time 500,000 operations in this browser" button in the portfolio's Tickbook scene
          (~/portfolio src/scenes/tickbook.ts, stream from src/tickbook/flow.ts timedStream, seed 1)

Workload: 20,000 resting limit orders placed first, not timed. Then 500,000 timed operations:
          50% limit orders that rest 1 to 50 ticks from the middle, 4% limit orders that cross by
          1 to 3 ticks, 3% market orders, 43% cancels, quantities 1 to 100. Same recipe as
          bench/bench.cpp but a different random number generator and a tenth of the length, so it
          is NOT the same stream as the native benchmark.
Timing:   performance.now() once before and once after the 500,000 operations. Each operation is
          one call from JavaScript into the module plus one call to count and clear the trade log.
          The stream is generated before the clock starts. A fresh book for every run.
